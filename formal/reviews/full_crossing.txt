1. SHIFTED TWO-COLUMN EMBEDDING

columnShift maps (r,k) in Fin m x Fin 2 to (r,c.val+k.val) in Fin m x Fin L,
where c : Fin (L-1). Its bound follows from c < L-1 and k < 2. Its injectivity
uses row equality and cancellation of the fixed natural column offset.
The left/right specialization lemmas identify its images with precisely
lowerCol c and upperCol c. No row rotation or longitudinal wrap is introduced.

A supplied cut already implies L >= 2. For L = 0 or 1 the cut type is empty,
so the universally quantified arbitrary-cut theorem has no nonexistent cut
to interpret. There is no hidden lower-bound hypothesis on L.

2. PHYSICAL MASKS REALLY GIVE THE CLAIMED EDGES

FullCrossingAt hm H c asserts, for every row r, both actual H-adjacencies
  (r,lowerCol c) -- (r,upperCol c), and
  (r,lowerCol c) -- (rowSucc hm r,upperCol c).
Thus it is the physical all-horizontal/all-diagonal crossing condition,
not a surrogate state or a condition on abstract frontier connectivity.

selectedId_adj passes from an actual selected ID through membership in
realizedEdges to fromEdgeSet adjacency. The ambient physical-edge proof
supplies distinct endpoints, as required by fromEdgeSet. The mask theorem
specializes this to the H and D IDs with their true Boolean bits. It therefore
establishes FullCrossingAt for the actual selected spanning graph.

Nonblocking prose clarity: selectedId_adj's comment about excluding
"diagonal pairs" means equal-endpoint pairs (self-loops). It does not exclude
the physical D-edge family. The proof itself is unambiguous and correct.

3. THE IMPORTED ZIGZAG IS A CONCRETE STANDARD CYCLE

ZigzagException.geometry sets all V bits false and all H/D bits true on two
columns. Its realized-edge and adjacency characterizations give exactly the
two physical families above, in both undirected orientations. The explicit
neighbor calculations give degree two; the row-successor argument connects
successive left vertices through a right vertex, and horizontal edges reach
every right vertex. The connected-two-regular theorem then supplies an
actual Mathlib Hamiltonian walk p in that selected graph.

The local cycle has length card (Fin m x Fin 2) = m*2. The source also proves
parity statements, but FullCrossing does not use them. This obstruction does
not depend on identifying parity with topological winding or cycle type.

4. HOMOMORPHISM INTO AN ARBITRARY CONNECTED TWO-REGULAR H

zigzagInto uses columnShift on vertices. Its adjacency proof unfolds the
exact zigzag edge characterization and handles both orientations using
FullCrossingAt and symmetry of H. zigzagInto_injective is exactly the shifted
vertex map's injectivity. Hence mapping the supplied local p gives a cycle
in H and preserves its length.

No premise H <= the ambient triangular-cylinder graph is needed. The proof
only requires H to contain these crossing edges. Any connected two-regular
finite simple graph containing this embedded cycle must be saturated by it,
even if H was initially presented without an ambient-edge restriction.

5. SATURATION RETAINS THE SAME SUPPLIED CYCLE

CycleSaturation.isHamiltonianCycle_of_neighbor_ncard_two accepts a particular
p : G.Walk v v together with p.IsCycle, G.Connected, and the degree-two
condition, and concludes p.IsHamiltonianCycle for that same p. It does not
replace p by another cycle whose length could be unrelated.

The proof obtains G.IsCycles from the degree condition, then uses the
supplied cycle's subgraph-closure property to propagate membership along
ambient reachability. Connectedness forces every vertex into that cycle's
subgraph. Its original support-tail nodup property and this coverage prove
Hamiltonicity. The subsequent length theorem therefore concerns the exact
input walk. The finite-type assumptions give ncard = 2 its intended ordinary
degree meaning.

6. LENGTH CANCELLATION AND MASK COROLLARIES

For the mapped local cycle, saturation gives
  p.length = card (Fin m x Fin L) = m*L,
while local Hamiltonicity gives p.length = m*2. Walk mapping preserves the
length. The final cancellation of m is valid because hm : 3 <= m implies
0 < m. The result is L = 2; there is no cancellation at m = 0 and no assumed
spanning property smuggled into the embedding step.

length_two_of_full_crossing_masks applies this result to the selected graph
of a HamiltonianGeometry, using its exact connected/two-regular predicate
and the bit-to-adjacency bridge. not_full_crossing_masks then contradicts
L >= 3. These are concrete mask/graph consequences at any supplied cut.
