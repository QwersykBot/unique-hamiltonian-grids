ALL-LENGTH SIGN AT CIRCUMFERENCE EIGHT FROM THE PUBLISHED RATIONAL FUNCTION
26 November 2026 — AI-authored mathematical supplement

Statement and source
--------------------
Use the counting convention of Bodroza-Pantic, Kwong and Pantic,
"A Conjecture on the Number of Hamiltonian Cycles on Thin Grid Cylinder
Graphs", DMTCS 17:1 (2015), 219–240:
https://dmtcs.episciences.org/en/articles/2113/download

Let h_c(n), h_nc(n) count the contractible and noncontractible undirected
Hamiltonian edge sets of C_8 x P_(n+1), on labelled vertices. Define

    a_k = (-1)^k [h_c(k+1) - h_nc(k+1)],  k >= 0.

Thus the vertex-column count is L=k+2. The conclusion is

    a_0 = 6, and a_(k+1) > 2 a_k > 0 for every k >= 0.

This proves the strict alternating-sign assertion in Question 4 (printed
page 238) at circumference eight for every length. It is not a theorem
for every even circumference.

Exact source data
-----------------
Section 6.7, printed page 235, gives K_8(x)=r_8(x)/s_8(x), where
K_8=H_8^c-H_8^nc. The companion verifier contains all coefficients of
r_8 and s_8, transcribed in ascending order, and explicitly substitutes
x -> -x. Therefore A(x)=sum_(k>=0) a_k x^k=P(x)/D(x), with
deg P=19, deg D=20 and D(0)=1.

The verifier expands exactly the twenty initial coefficients a_0,...,a_19
by solving D(x)A(x)=P(x). It separately contains all fifteen coefficients
printed for each of H_8^c and H_8^nc, and compares their alternating
differences with the first fifteen expanded coefficients. These checks
support the transcription; the all-length conclusion comes from the
following recurrence argument.

Positive recurrence proof
-------------------------
Let d(t)=t^20 D(1/t). Exact integer synthetic division gives

    d(t)=(t-2) Q(t)-gamma,
    Q(t)=t^19-beta_1*t^18-...-beta_19,
    gamma=117455600,

where the beta list, in this order, is

    3, 110, 749, 3046, 7922, 11929, 10331, 13480, 47874,
    126775, 244336, 453635, 908475, 1836540, 3673970,
    7342170, 14682292, 29363996, 58727808.

Every beta is positive. Because deg P<20, the coefficients of P/D obey
d(E)a_k=0 for every k>=0, where E shifts a sequence one place forward.
Put b_k=a_(k+1)-2 a_k. Substituting the displayed factorization yields

    b_(k+19)=beta_1 b_(k+18)+...+beta_19 b_k+gamma a_k.

The verifier checks a_0=6 and b_0,...,b_18>0 directly from the exact
rational expansion. Their minimum is 178. These premises imply
a_0,...,a_19>0 by a_(k+1)=2a_k+b_k. Joint induction now proves every
subsequent b positive using the recurrence, and every subsequent a
positive using that same update identity. The conclusion therefore
holds for all k, without checking or estimating further terms.
